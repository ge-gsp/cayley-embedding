Cayley Embedding Workbench

Paste a graph. Get the certified brackets for the three embedding routes — Cayley completion, isometric, induced — and the route that fits what you intend to do with it. Everything runs in your browser.

Brackets follow the certification convention of the GE-GSP framework: a value is shown as exact only where both directions are certified, otherwise as a bracket. The degree bracket and the counting floor are the two genuinely linear-time screens; the diameter floor costs a BFS from every vertex and the local order floor needs an independence number per neighbourhood. γ⁺ and γ△ are exact up to 10 vertices in this browser: the census of all 995 connected graphs on two to seven is carried in the page, and beyond it the same search runs live over every abelian group of order n.
